# Digital Forensics CTF Walkthroughs

> Educational walkthroughs of Capture The Flag (CTF) digital forensics challenges, demonstrating investigative methodology, evidence analysis, and forensic reasoning.

---

## Overview

This repository contains narrated walkthroughs of digital forensics Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges completed during my computer science studies. Rather than simply presenting solutions, these videos explain the investigative process used to identify, analyze, and validate digital evidence.

The goal is to demonstrate the thought process behind forensic investigations, including artifact analysis, evidence collection, and systematic problem solving.

## About CTFs

Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges are hands-on cybersecurity exercises designed to simulate real-world investigations. Digital forensics CTFs require participants to analyze evidence, recover artifacts, identify indicators of compromise, and apply investigative techniques similar to those used in incident response and forensic examinations.
